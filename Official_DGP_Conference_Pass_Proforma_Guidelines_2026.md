# Police Personnel Duty Pass Registration Proforma – Instructions

Please fill out the spreadsheet according to the column instructions below. This proforma is strictly for police personnel, officers, and staff deployed for the 61st All India DGPs / IGPs Conference 2026. Do not alter or delete column headers.

---

### 1. Column-by-Column Instructions

| Column | Header Name | Description | Example |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Sl. No.** | Sequence number. | `1`, `2`, `3` |
| **B** | **Full Name** | Full legal name with rank prefix or title. | `Sri K. Ramesh Babu, IPS`<br>`M. Suresh Kumar` |
| **C** | **Father's / Spouse's Name** | Father's or spouse's name for security verification. | `Sri K. Subba Rao` |
| **D** | **District** | Sending police district name. | `Visakhapatnam`<br>`Guntur`<br>`Chittoor`<br>`Nellore`<br>`Tirupati` |
| **E** | **Designation / Rank** | Official police rank, trade, or role. | `Additional SP`<br>`Sub-Inspector`<br>`Police Constable`<br>`Driver`<br>`Wireless Operator` |
| **F** | **Mobile Number** | 10-digit mobile number only (no `+91`, no spaces, no dashes). | `9848012345` |
| **G** | **Aadhaar Number** | 12-digit Aadhaar number (enter as Text so Excel preserves all digits). | `270625123456` |
| **H** | **Photograph Reference** | Photo filename (e.g. `9848012345.jpg`), Drive link, or **leave blank** if photo will be captured on arrival. | `9848012345.jpg` |

---

### 2. Important Data Guidelines

* **Mobile Numbers**: Must contain exactly 10 digits (do not enter `+91`, `0`, or hyphens).
* **Aadhaar Numbers**: Must contain exactly 12 numeric digits. Enter the cell format as **Text** in Excel (or prefix with `'`) so that 12-digit numbers are not converted to scientific notation (e.g. `2.70625E+11`).
* **District (Column D)**: Specify the police district name (e.g. `Visakhapatnam`, `Guntur`, `Chittoor`, `Nellore`, `Kadapa`, `Tirupati`, `Kurnool`, `Ananthapuramu`). All submissions under this proforma are automatically classified under the Police Department.

---

### 3. Photograph Guidelines & Spot Capture

* **If photos are available**: Name each photo file with the person's **10-digit mobile number** (e.g. `9848012345.jpg`), or provide a direct link in Column H.
* **If photos are not available**: **Leave Column H blank**. When the officer or personnel reports at the registration desk, their photograph will be captured on the spot via camera and printed on their official duty pass immediately.
